1Core Concepts 核心概念

Explicit Cost 显性成本

The direct expense of purchasing factors of production involves actual cash payment.

实际支付出去的钱：房租、原材料、工资、水电等看得见的支出。

Implicit Cost 隐性成本

The opportunity cost of using self-owned factors of production involves no cash payment.

使用自有资源产生的机会成本，比如开店放弃的打工工资。

Accounting Profit 会计利润

Accounting profit is the paper profit obtained by subtracting only explicit costs from total revenue.

账面利润，只扣显性成本。

Economic Profit 经济利润

Economic profit equals total revenue minus both explicit and implicit costs.

同时扣显性成本与隐性成本，反映真实经济结果。

Accounting Profit = Total Revenue − Explicit Cost
会计利润 = 总收益 − 显性成本
Economic Profit = Total Revenue − Explicit Cost − Implicit Cost
经济利润 = 总收益 − 显性成本 − 隐性成本

5Critical Thinking 批判性思考
"Accounting profit overstates the true profitability of a business." Evaluate this statement.

"会计利润高估了企业的真实盈利能力。"评价这个说法。

Accounting profit only deducts explicit cost. It ignores implicit cost (opportunity cost). Economic profit = Revenue − Explicit − Implicit, so it is always lower than accounting profit. Thus accounting profit overstates true profitability.

会计利润只扣显性成本，忽略了隐性成本（机会成本）。经济利润=总收益−显性−隐性，所以一定比会计利润低。因此会计利润确实高估了真实盈利能力。
